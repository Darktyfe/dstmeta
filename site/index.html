    How losses work

    A plan is rejected if any final stat drops below the negative of its permissible loss. Keep the defaults at 0 for a no-loss plan, or raise a limit to trade one stat for a stronger result in another.

    What gets considered

    Eating raw, roasting first, drying on a rack (unless ignored), keeping an item, or cooking four items in the pot. Plans are checked as a whole, not dish by dish. Slow, over-time stat gains are skipped unless enabled.
